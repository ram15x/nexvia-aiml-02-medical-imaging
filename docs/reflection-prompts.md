# Individual reflection prompts

This reflection must describe the student's real work and learning. Draft it in the student's own words after reviewing the finished code and evidence; do not claim work that was not personally performed.

- What parts did I implement and verify myself?
- What did I learn about patient-level splitting, report-mined labels, and leakage?
- Which modeling or engineering choice changed after validation, and why?
- What failed or took longer than expected? How did I debug it?
- What are the strongest limitations of this prototype?
- What would I change with more time, data, and expert review?
- Which artifacts or commits show my individual contribution?
